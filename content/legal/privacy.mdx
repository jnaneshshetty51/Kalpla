**Data Fiduciary**: Learncap Academy Pvt Ltd ("Kalpla," "we," "us")

This policy is written under India's **Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDPA)**. Under the DPDPA, we are a "Data Fiduciary" and you are a "Data Principal" — the terms used throughout this policy.

## 1. What we collect

- **Account information**: name, email, and any profile details you provide.
- **Learning and competency data**: your diagnostic scores, skill-node progress, and competency scores across the platform's skill categories.
- **Submissions**: code you write and submit, project reflections, and assessment answers.
- **AI tutor conversations**: your messages to the AI tutor and its responses, stored to maintain conversation context and improve grading accuracy.
- **Payment information**: for platform subscriptions, payment is processed by Stripe — we do not store your card details ourselves. For Pilot Cohort programs, payment may be collected via other methods (e.g. UPI or bank transfer) and reconciled by our team.
- **Cohort communication and attendance data** (Pilot Cohort programs only): information shared through the cohort's communication channel and session attendance.
- **Outcomes data** (Pilot Cohort programs only, with consent): post-cohort employment status, if you choose to share it.

## 2. Why we process your data

We only use your data for purposes you'd reasonably expect from using Kalpla: operating your account, generating your personalized roadmap, grading your work, running any cohort you're enrolled in, processing payments, and communicating with you about the program. We do not sell your personal data.

## 3. AI processing — a specific disclosure

To power the AI tutor and AI-assisted grading, your code submissions, questions, and responses are sent to third-party AI model providers (**OpenAI** and **Anthropic**) for processing. This is a real, active data flow — every AI tutor conversation and AI-graded submission is processed this way.

## 4. Who we share data with (third parties)

| Party | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Supabase | Database hosting and authentication |
| OpenAI, Anthropic | AI tutor responses and AI-assisted grading |
| Stripe | Payment processing for platform subscriptions |
| Resend | Transactional email delivery |

We don't share your personal data with anyone beyond what's needed to run these services.

## 5. Your rights as a Data Principal

Under the DPDPA, you have the right to:

- Access a summary of the personal data we hold about you
- Request correction of inaccurate data
- Request erasure of your data, subject to our legitimate need to retain records (e.g. payment records for accounting purposes)
- Withdraw consent for optional processing (e.g. testimonial use, outcomes-data sharing) at any time
- Nominate another individual to exercise these rights on your behalf in the event of death or incapacity

To exercise any of these rights, contact us through the app.

## 6. Grievance Officer

As required under the DPDPA, a named Grievance Officer will be published here once formally designated. Until then, direct any data-rights request or grievance through the app's support channel.

## 7. Data retention

We retain your data for as long as your account is active, plus a reasonable period afterward for legal, accounting, and dispute-resolution purposes.

## 8. Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your data. No system is perfectly secure, and we can't guarantee absolute security.

## 9. Children's data

Kalpla is not directed at children under 18. We don't knowingly collect data from anyone under the age of majority.

## 10. Cookies and analytics

The platform does not currently integrate a third-party analytics or advertising tool. If that changes, this section will be updated to disclose it.

## 11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be communicated via the app.

## 12. Governing law

This policy is governed by the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, and other applicable laws of India.
