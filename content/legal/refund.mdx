**Operated by**: Learncap Academy Pvt Ltd ("Kalpla," "we," "us")

This policy applies to Kalpla Pilot Cohort programs (cohort-based accelerators billed as a fixed program fee). Self-serve platform subscriptions are billed separately through Stripe and can be cancelled anytime from the billing page — see the [Terms of Service](/terms).

## 1. Cooling-off period

You may cancel your enrollment for any reason within **7 days of your enrollment date, or before the cohort's official start date (whichever is later)**, for a full refund of any amount paid.

To cancel, contact us through the app or the cohort's communication channel.

## 2. Refund schedule after the cooling-off period

If you withdraw after the cooling-off period, refunds are prorated against how much of the program you've completed, based on the cohort's published month structure:

| Withdrawal point | Refund |
|---|---|
| Before Month 2 begins | 75% of amount paid |
| Before Month 3 begins | 50% of amount paid |
| Before Month 4 begins | 25% of amount paid |
| Month 4 onward | No refund |

This schedule reflects that live sessions, mentor time, and grading capacity are committed and consumed as the cohort progresses.

## 3. Installment plans and withdrawal

Where a program fee is split into installments, if you withdraw:

- Any **future, not-yet-due installments are cancelled** — you owe nothing further.
- Any **already-paid installments** are refunded according to the schedule in Section 2, applied to the total amount actually paid so far.

Installment plans are a direct payment arrangement between you and us. They are **not a loan, EMI, or BNPL product** — no third-party lender is involved, and no interest is charged, unless a specific program explicitly states otherwise.

## 4. If we cancel or postpone a cohort

If we cancel a program entirely, or postpone its start by more than 30 days, you're entitled to a **full refund** of everything paid, regardless of Section 2's schedule.

## 5. How refunds are processed

Refunds are issued to the original payment method within **10 business days** of an approved refund request.
