# AI Practice vs. Human Mock Interviews — What's What

*This document is written to be reusable directly in student-facing materials (welcome packet, FAQ) — the language below is meant to be copy-pasteable, not just internal reference.*

Kalpla combines an always-on AI tutor with real human mentors. It's easy to assume "AI-powered platform" means everything is automated, including interview prep. **It doesn't — not yet.** Here's exactly what's AI and what's human in this pilot cohort.

## The AI tutor (available 24/7, part of the platform)

- Answers your questions and unblocks you when you're stuck, any time
- Reviews your code and gives you a grade with a confidence score attached — not a black box
- Generates practice questions tied to your personal skill roadmap
- Tracks your progress across all 24 skill nodes and tells you what to work on next
- Gives you daily coaching nudges based on where you actually are

None of this simulates a live interview conversation. It's practice, feedback, and coaching — not interview roleplay.

## Human mock interviews (live, scheduled, part of the pilot cohort)

**In this pilot cohort, every mock interview is conducted live by a human mentor.** There is no automated AI-run mock interview feature in the product yet — the platform's own pricing page already flags this honestly ("mock interviews — coming soon" next to the automated feature). This document is the fuller explanation behind that one-line note.

- **What it is**: a real, scheduled conversation with a human mentor simulating a real GenAI Engineer interview — technical questions, system-design discussion, and behavioral questions.
- **When**: concentrated in the job-readiness phase of the cohort (Month 5–6), not spread out weekly — see [`01`](01-weekly-session-calendar.md). Two rounds per student.
- **How you book it**: through the cohort WhatsApp group / shared schedule — see [`08`](../operational/08-communication-tool-decision.md).
- **What you get**: live verbal feedback in the session, plus a short written follow-up note from the mentor.

## Why this distinction matters

Being clear about this upfront avoids two bad outcomes: students showing up expecting an AI-run mock interview feature that doesn't exist, and Kalpla implicitly overstating what the AI does. Neither serves the cohort. If/when an automated AI mock-interview feature ships on the platform, this document — and the pricing page's note — should be updated together, not separately (avoid the kind of copy drift already found elsewhere in the app; see the README's [open items](../README.md#open-items-need-a-human-decision-not-more-drafting)).

**A deliberate choice**: this document does not quote specific monthly usage numbers for the AI tutor (e.g. "X messages/month") — those numbers live in the app's tier configuration and can change without this document being updated, which is exactly the kind of drift risk already found elsewhere in the codebase. If you need the current number, check `/pricing` on the live app, not this document.
