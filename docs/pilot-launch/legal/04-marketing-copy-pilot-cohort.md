# Marketing Copy — Pilot Cohort 1 Outreach & Landing Page

This document has two audiences: Section 0 is internal (compliance reasoning), Sections 1–2 are meant to be lifted directly into real outreach/landing pages, Section 3 is an internal audit trail. Read Section 0 before editing Sections 1–2 — the `[COMPLIANCE NOTE]` callouts explain *why* a claim is worded the way it is, so a future edit doesn't accidentally undo the reasoning.

## Section 0 — Compliance rules for this cohort

Applicable frameworks: ASCI (Advertising Standards Council of India) guidelines on truthful/non-misleading advertising, and the Consumer Protection Act 2019 + E-Commerce Rules 2020 (total price disclosure, no dark-pattern urgency).

**Hard rules — no exceptions:**
1. **Never promise a job or a placement outcome.** "Guaranteed placement," "100% placement," "get hired in 90 days" — none of this. Use "placement **assistance**," "we help you get hired," "we maximize your chances."
2. **No income or salary figures.** Cohort 1 has zero outcomes data (see [`10`](../operational/10-outcomes-tracking-template.md)'s compliance note) — there's nothing truthful to cite yet, and even later, individual comp figures need separate consent.
3. **No fabricated or unconsented testimonials.** Only use a testimonial if the `10` tracking sheet shows real, explicit written consent for that specific student.
4. **Show the total price and payment terms plainly wherever payment is solicited** — ₹49,000, 3-installment plan, with a link to the [refund policy](05-refund-cancellation-policy.md). Never bury pricing behind a "contact us" wall.
5. **No fake urgency.** If "limited seats" is used, the number must be a real, current seat count — not a manufactured countdown.
6. **Don't reuse the platform's "90 days" tagline for this cohort.** It describes a different, self-paced product experience. This is a "6-month guided cohort" — see [`01`](../curriculum/01-weekly-session-calendar.md)'s note on why the pace differs.
7. **Don't overstate the AI.** Mock interviews are human-delivered, not AI-run — see [`02`](../curriculum/02-ai-vs-human-mock-interviews.md). Say what's actually true.

## Section 2 — Landing page copy

### Hero

> **Become a GenAI Engineer. Build the proof, not just the theory.**
> A 6-month, project-based accelerator combining an AI-powered learning platform with real human mentorship. Kalpla Pilot Cohort 1.

`[COMPLIANCE NOTE: "Become a GenAI Engineer" describes the program's goal/design, not a guaranteed personal outcome — paired immediately with "build the proof," which is specific and true (3 real graded projects). Deliberately does not reuse the platform's own "90 days" homepage tagline — see Rule 6.]`

### Who this is for

> For people who want to become a GenAI Engineer and are willing to put in the work — self-motivated learners, early-career developers, and career switchers with basic programming familiarity. This is a small, mentor-supported cohort, not a self-serve course.

### How it works

> **AI + human, working together.**
> Kalpla's AI tutor is available 24/7 — it answers your questions, reviews your code against real production rubrics, and gives you a confidence-scored grade, not a black box. Your personal roadmap adapts to what you actually know.
>
> Real humans handle what AI can't: weekly live classes, biweekly architecture deep-dives, career mentorship, and — importantly — **mock interviews are conducted live by a human mentor, not an AI feature.** [Read the full breakdown of what's AI vs. human →](02-ai-vs-human-mock-interviews.md) — *(link to the reusable version of that document, once published)*

### What you'll build

> Three real, portfolio-ready projects, each reviewed against a genuine GenAI engineering rubric — not just "does it run":
> - **Prompt Playground** — foundational prompt-engineering and API integration
> - **RAG Chatbot** — a real retrieval-augmented generation system
> - **Tool-Using Agent** — an agent that reasons about which tools to call and when
>
> Every project gets real feedback: correctness, readability, edge-case handling, and GenAI-specific criteria like retrieval quality and tool-use correctness — the same rubric shape mentors use to grade this pilot cohort by hand.

`[COMPLIANCE NOTE: only the 3 real seeded ladder projects are named — never the business-vision doc's aspirational 5-project list (AI SaaS, AI Customer Support, etc.), which doesn't exist on the platform. See [`01`](../curriculum/01-weekly-session-calendar.md).]`

### Placement assistance

> We help you get hired — we don't guarantee it, and we won't pretend to. What we do provide, concretely:
> - Resume and LinkedIn review with a human mentor
> - Portfolio and GitHub review
> - 2 live mock interviews with detailed feedback
> - Employer referrals and introductions where we have them
>
> This is our first cohort, so we don't have placement statistics to show you yet — we'll publish real, honestly-reported outcomes starting with future cohorts.

`[COMPLIANCE NOTE: this section is the single most important one for ASCI compliance — explicit "we don't guarantee it" framing, a concrete/provable list of services (Rule 1), and an explicit acknowledgment of no outcomes data yet (Rule 2) rather than staying silent about it, which could read as evasive.]`
