# Refund & Cancellation Policy — GenAI Engineer Accelerator, Pilot Cohort 1

**Operated by**: Learncap Academy Pvt Ltd ("Kalpla," "we," "us")
**Program**: GenAI Engineer Accelerator, Pilot Cohort 1
**Price**: ₹49,000, payable as a 3-installment plan (~₹17,000 × 3), paid directly to us — not a third-party loan or EMI product. See [why](../README.md#why-installment-plan-not-emi).

## 1. Cooling-off period

You may cancel your enrollment for any reason within **7 days of your enrollment date, or before the cohort's official start date (whichever is later)**, for a full refund of any amount paid. This is a policy we've chosen to offer, not a claim that it's separately required by a specific statute — we're stating it plainly so it isn't unclear.

To cancel, message us on the cohort WhatsApp group or contact `[SUPPORT EMAIL — TBD]`.

## 2. Refund schedule after the cooling-off period

If you withdraw after the cooling-off period, refunds are prorated against how much of the 6-month program you've completed, based on the [month structure in the program calendar](../curriculum/01-weekly-session-calendar.md):

| Withdrawal point | Refund |
|---|---|
| Before Month 2 begins | 75% of amount paid |
| Before Month 3 begins | 50% of amount paid |
| Before Month 4 begins | 25% of amount paid |
| Month 4 onward | No refund |

This schedule reflects that live sessions, mentor time, and manual grading capacity are committed and consumed as the cohort progresses — it isn't possible to "un-deliver" a live class or a completed project review.

## 3. Installment plan and withdrawal

Your ₹49,000 is split into 3 installments so you don't have to pay the full amount upfront. If you withdraw:
- Any **future, not-yet-due installments are cancelled** — you owe nothing further.
- Any **already-paid installments** are refunded according to the schedule in Section 2, applied to the total amount actually paid so far.

This is a direct payment plan between you and us. It is **not a loan, EMI, or BNPL product** — no third-party lender is involved, and no interest is charged.

## 4. If we cancel or postpone the cohort

This is a first pilot cohort, and we want to be upfront that first-cohort execution risk is real. If we cancel the program entirely, or postpone its start by more than 30 days, you're entitled to a **full refund** of everything paid, regardless of Section 2's schedule.

## 5. How refunds are processed

Refunds are issued to the original payment method (UPI or bank transfer — see [payment collection method](../operational/09-payment-collection-decision.md)) within **10 business days** of an approved refund request. Because payments are currently collected and reconciled manually (not through an automated payment gateway), refunds are also manual — there is no instant/automated refund button today, and this document does not promise one.

This policy is governed by the laws of India. Jurisdiction: Udupi, Karnataka.
