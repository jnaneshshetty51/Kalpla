# Privacy Policy — Kalpla

**Data Fiduciary**: Learncap Academy Pvt Ltd ("Kalpla," "we," "us")

This policy is written under India's **Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDPA)**. Under the DPDPA, we are a "Data Fiduciary" and you are a "Data Principal" — the terms used throughout this policy.

## 1. What we collect

- **Account information**: name, email, and any profile details you provide.
- **Learning and competency data**: your diagnostic scores, skill-node progress, and competency scores across the 8 skill categories.
- **Submissions**: code you write and submit, project reflections, and assessment answers.
- **AI tutor conversations**: your messages to the AI tutor and its responses, stored to maintain conversation context and improve grading accuracy.
- **Payment information**: for platform subscriptions, payment is processed by Stripe — we do not store your card details ourselves. For Pilot Cohort 1, payment is currently collected via UPI/bank transfer and reconciled manually (see [`09`](../operational/09-payment-collection-decision.md)) — this means payment reference numbers and transaction confirmations are handled by a named team member, not an automated payment processor.
- **Cohort communication and attendance data** (Pilot Cohort 1 only): your phone number as shared in the cohort WhatsApp group, session attendance, and mock-interview scheduling — see [`08`](../operational/08-communication-tool-decision.md).
- **Outcomes data** (Pilot Cohort 1 only, with consent): post-cohort employment status, if you choose to share it — see [`10`](../operational/10-outcomes-tracking-template.md).

## 2. Why we process your data (purpose limitation)

We only use your data for purposes you'd reasonably expect from using Kalpla: operating your account, generating your personalized roadmap, grading your work, running the cohort, processing payments, and communicating with you about the program. We do not sell your personal data.

## 3. AI processing — a specific disclosure

To power the AI tutor and AI-assisted grading, your code submissions, questions, and responses are sent to third-party AI model providers (**OpenAI** and **Anthropic**) for processing. This is a real, active data flow, not a hypothetical one — every AI tutor conversation and AI-graded submission is processed this way. For Pilot Cohort 1 specifically, project and assessment grading is currently done manually by human mentors rather than sent through the AI grader (see [`03`](../curriculum/03-manual-grading-rubrics.md)), so this disclosure applies most directly to AI tutor usage during the pilot.

## 4. Who we share data with (third parties)

| Party | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Supabase | Database hosting and authentication |
| OpenAI, Anthropic | AI tutor responses and AI-assisted grading |
| Stripe | Payment processing for platform subscriptions |
| Resend | Transactional email delivery |
| WhatsApp (Meta) | Pilot Cohort 1 group communication — your phone number becomes visible to other cohort members in the group |

We don't share your personal data with anyone beyond what's needed to run these services.

## 5. Your rights as a Data Principal

Under the DPDPA, you have the right to:
- Access a summary of the personal data we hold about you
- Request correction of inaccurate data
- Request erasure of your data, subject to our legitimate need to retain records (e.g. payment records for accounting purposes)
- Withdraw consent for optional processing (e.g. testimonial use, outcomes-data sharing) at any time
- Nominate another individual to exercise these rights on your behalf in the event of death or incapacity

To exercise any of these rights, contact our Grievance Officer below.
